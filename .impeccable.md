# Impeccable — LoloShop

This file holds the locked design context for LoloShop. All design skills read this before doing work. Do **not** use the current/shipped website design as reference or inspiration — it is being deliberately replaced.

## Design Context

### Users
- **Students (retail, 16–25)** — phone-only. The premium emotional core: they design a custom graduation sash, browse gowns/caps, and want to present themselves beautifully at a once-in-a-lifetime moment. They care about style, memories, elegance, looking premium.
- **Wholesalers (ممثل جامعة)** — phone-only. Manage 100+ students, approvals, deadlines. Want speed and confidence, not decoration — but still inside the brand world.
- **Staff (موظف)** — iPad-first. View orders + completed design files/attachments. Utilitarian but on-brand.
- **Admin (مدير)** — laptop-first. Profits, costs, orders, wholesaler control. Dense data, on-brand.
- **Language:** Arabic-only, `dir="rtl"` `lang="ar"`. Mobile-first for student + wholesaler always.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **couture, cinematic, composed.**
Should feel like a premium graduation *fashion house*, not an ecommerce store. Emotion goals: pride, elegance, anticipation, the weight of a milestone. Calm and confident, never loud or salesy. No urgency-marketing, no "Shopify section" stacking, no startup gloss.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme: Light editorial.** Warm paper canvas, ink type, generous negative space — a print fashion catalog / lookbook, not a dashboard.
- **Palette — neutral-luxury foundation, orange as rare spark** (my call, within brand constraints):
  - Canvas / paper: `#FAF4EA` warm paper, surfaces `#FFFDFA` → `#F5F0E6`
  - Ink: `#1A1A1A`, soft ink `#33302B`, muted `#8A8377`
  - Accent (rare, emotional peaks only — CTAs, the one number that matters, active state): burnt orange `#C2410C`; brand fill orange `#F47B42` reserved for large fills/the logo mark.
  - Lead with ink + paper + warm neutrals; orange appears at <=10% visual weight. Brand stays @lolo_shop96-warm but disciplined.
  - Use OKLCH for tints; reduce chroma toward extremes; tint neutrals toward the warm orange hue (very low chroma).
- **Type (already wired, keep):** Arabic UI/body **Cairo**; Arabic display/calligraphy **Amiri**; Latin display **Playfair Display**; logo/flourish script **Great Vibes** (logo only). Lean on Amiri at display sizes for couture Arabic headings. Fewer sizes, more contrast (>=1.25 ratio), fluid clamp on headings.
- **Anti-references (hard):** no Shopify-style stacked sections, no card-grid monotony, no startup/trendy aesthetic, no generic AI-slop layouts, no current LoloShop design. No gradient text (the shipped `.text-gradient-brand` is banned — replace with solid ink/orange + weight). No glassmorphism-everywhere, no >1px colored side-stripe accents.
- **No specific positive references requested** — execute the brief on judgment.

### Scope
**Everything** gets the premium language, prioritized:
1. **Student storefront + sash customization** — the signature moment; heaviest craft (home, shop, product, designer, sizes).
2. Wholesaler (phone-first, fast, on-brand).
3. Staff (iPad) + Admin (laptop) — editorial restraint applied to dense/data UI; legibility and density win over decoration, but type/color/space stay on the brand system.

### Design Principles
1. **Lookbook, not storefront.** Product as art: big imagery, negative space, type-led composition. Browsing should feel like turning pages, not scrolling a catalog of identical cards.
2. **Orange is earned.** Ink + warm paper carry the page; burnt orange appears only at emotional/action peaks. Rarity = power.
3. **Arabic type is the craft.** Amiri at display scale, calligraphic confidence — heritage-meets-couture. Hierarchy through contrast, not noise.
4. **The sash designer is the soul.** The customization experience must feel premium and tactile, the thing people remember.
5. **Restraint scales to data.** Admin/staff dashboards stay on the system but prioritize legibility and density — no decoration that hurts the job.
6. **Mobile-first, RTL-true at every breakpoint.** Tap targets >=44px, no h-scroll, never amputate features on phone.
