# Product

## Register

brand

The signature surface is the student storefront + sash customization: design IS the product there. Dashboards (admin/staff/wholesaler) inherit the brand system but get product-register treatment per task. Default register is `brand`; override to `product` when working a dashboard/data surface.

## Users

- **Students (retail, 21–25)** — phone-only, Arabic. The premium emotional core: they design a custom graduation sash and browse gowns/caps for a once-in-a-lifetime moment. They care about style, memories, elegance, presenting themselves in a premium way. Job: feel proud of how they'll look, and make it real with little friction.
- **Wholesalers (ممثل جامعة)** — phone-only. Manage 100+ students, approvals, deadlines. Want speed and confidence, inside the brand world but not slowed by decoration.
- **Staff (موظف)** — iPad-first. View orders + completed design files/attachments. Utilitarian, on-brand.
- **Admin (مدير)** — laptop-first. Profits, costs, orders, wholesaler control. Dense data, on-brand.
- **Language:** Arabic-only, `dir="rtl"` `lang="ar"`. Mobile-first for student + wholesaler always.

## Product Purpose

LoloShop is a graduation fashion brand and platform: sells graduation gowns, caps, and custom-designed sashes (أوشحة تخرج), and runs the operation behind them. Students design their own sash online; wholesalers manage groups of students with approvals and deadlines; admins track orders, costs, and profit. Payments are cash only. Success = the storefront feels like a premium graduation fashion house (not an ecommerce store) so students convert on pride and elegance, while the dashboards stay fast and legible enough to run 100+ student batches without friction.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **couture, cinematic, composed.** A premium graduation fashion house, not a Shopify store. Emotional goals: pride, elegance, anticipation, the weight of a milestone. Voice is calm and confident, never loud or salesy. No urgency-marketing, no section-stacking, no startup gloss. Arabic-first; the Arabic type IS the craft.

## Anti-references

Do **not** use the current/shipped LoloShop design as reference or inspiration — it is being deliberately replaced. Avoid:
- Shopify-style stacked sections; card-grid monotony (identical icon+heading+text tiles repeated).
- Generic AI-slop layouts; trendy startup/SaaS aesthetic; clutter.
- Scrim-over-photo "AI" product cards — use caption-below editorial tiles instead.
- Gradient text (the shipped `.text-gradient-brand` is banned — solid ink/orange + weight contrast).
- Glassmorphism-everywhere; colored side-stripe accents >1px; urgency/marketing-buzzword copy.
No specific positive references were requested — execute on judgment.

## Design Principles

1. **Lookbook, not storefront.** Product as art: big imagery, negative space, type-led composition. Browsing should feel like turning pages, not scrolling identical cards.
2. **Orange is earned.** Ink + warm paper carry the page; burnt orange appears only at emotional/action peaks. Rarity = power (≤10% visual weight).
3. **Arabic type is the craft.** Amiri at display scale, calligraphic confidence — heritage-meets-couture. Hierarchy through contrast, not noise.
4. **The sash designer is the soul.** The customization experience must feel premium and tactile — the thing people remember.
5. **Restraint scales to data.** Admin/staff dashboards stay on the system but prioritize legibility and density; no decoration that hurts the job.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG AA.** Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, placeholders held to the same bar (watch muted warm-neutral body on warm-paper — the easy failure here).
- Tap targets ≥44px; no horizontal scroll; RTL-true at every breakpoint; never amputate features on phone.
- **Reduced motion:** every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade or instant).
